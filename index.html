Less chasing. Three clear steps.

01

One person pays.

Add electricity, gas or broadband. Choose who paid. The total is split into five shares, down to the cent.

02

Everyone knows their part.

Pick a housemate’s view to see what they owe and to whom. Copy a reminder when someone needs a nudge.

03

Record it. Close the loop.

After repayment, the bill payer records it as received. That share is cleared and the balance updates. Mistake? Undo it.

What this prototype demonstrates: splitting bills, tracking unpaid shares, recording repayments and preparing reminders. It runs in one browser with fictional data. Switching names simulates different housemates; it is not a login. A real shared service needs secure accounts and data that syncs across devices. Payment entries here are manual records, not bank verification.
